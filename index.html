Case Study

Medical Equipment Reconditioner — quantifying a circular business model for investor and acquirer diligence

Mass-balanced 269 tonnes of equipment throughput across a portfolio company's medical equipment reconditioning operations, benchmarked their circularity and emissions performance against industry, and identified roughly $2–3M in annual cost avoidance from component reuse alongside a validated $9.5M reconditioned-equipment revenue stream — delivered as an ISSB-aligned report ahead of investor review.

How an engagement runs

01

Data collection

Procurement, production, and waste records — gathered from what you already have.

02

Flow mapping

Inputs and outputs mapped at process level, mass-balanced across operations.

03

Analysis

Circularity indicators, resource efficiency metrics, and ESRS E5 data points derived from the model.

04

Deliverables

A disclosure-ready dataset, visual flow diagrams, and a plain-language report.
